
PDF/DOCX Page Layout Requirements

- Let users choose A4 or US Letter. Default to A4, show the selected size, and let users change it before export.
- Keep the preview consistent with the exported file’s page size and layout. Show the page count before download.
- Use readable, consistent typography, spacing, indentation, and margins. Offer simple presets such as comfortable, balanced (default), and compact.
- Balance whitespace against page count. Avoid both crowded pages and large blank areas. Don’t shrink text below a readable size just to fit a preferred page count.
- Use automatic page flow by default. Let users optionally start selected major sections on a new page; don’t force every section onto its own page.
- Keep section headings with the content that follows. Avoid splitting a single experience, education, certification, or project entry across pages when practical.
- Keep bullets with their text, and keep dates with the associated role or qualification. Avoid stranded labels and nearly empty final pages where practical.
- For one-page requests that don’t fit legibly, offer layout adjustments or content review. Don’t silently remove content or compress it excessively.
- Preserve selectable text in PDFs and editable text, headings, and lists in DOCX. Check that fonts, links, and special characters render correctly.
- Provide a preview before download and let users return to content or layout settings without losing their work.
